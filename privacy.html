Privacy Policy

Hauligan · last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are. BLK MKT LLC makes Hauligan. Contact: will@blkmkt.app.

Hauligan is a game about packing a truck and driving it. It has no accounts of its own, no chat and no user-generated content. This page explains the little data the game touches.

What the game stores on your device

Hauligan keeps your progress (stars, best times, coins, streaks, garage items), your settings (volumes, vibration, reduced motion, high contrast) and which purchases you own. All of it stays in the app's own storage on your device. We never see it, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Hauligan is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads for HINT, DOUBLE IT!, INSURANCE and free coins play only when you choose them, and short ads may play between orders. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects data such as:

  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • which ads you were shown and how you interacted with them;
  • crash logs and performance data about the app.

Google uses this data for advertising and analytics as described in Google's privacy policy and its data disclosure for the AdMob SDK. We don't receive this data.

Your choices. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt controls whether the advertising identifier is shared, and you can change it any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they aren't personalised.

Purchases

In-app purchases (No Forced Ads, the Starter Pack and coin packs) are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers how it handles purchases.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Hauligan sends your total stars and best daily streak to its leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy, and you can sign out in your device's Settings.

Children

Hauligan is family-friendly, but it isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Analytics and sharing

Hauligan has no analytics of its own. We don't sell personal data. The only third parties that receive data from the app are Google, for the advertising described above, and Apple, for purchases and Game Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to its use. We hold no personal data on our own servers, so these requests mostly concern Google; its privacy policy explains how to make them. For anything else, email will@blkmkt.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
